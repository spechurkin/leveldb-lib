# MultiLevelDB Code of Conduct

Everyone should be able to participate in MultiLevelDB without harassment or discrimination. This code applies to all participants, including maintainers, in project spaces such as issues, pull requests, and discussions. It also applies when someone publicly represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree or when someone is new to the project.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work and consider feedback in good faith.
- Respect other participants' privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, slurs, discriminatory remarks, or personal attacks.
- Unwanted sexual attention, remarks, or imagery.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Retaliation against someone for reporting an incident or helping review a report.

## Reporting and moderation

Report incidents privately through the [GitHub abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). If GitHub offers **Report to repository admins**, you can use it to reach the maintainers. You can also report abuse directly to GitHub Support. Include links and enough context to identify what happened. Keep sensitive details out of public issues and pull requests.

Maintainers will handle reports they receive discreetly and limit access to identifying details to people who need them to respond. Depending on the severity and whether the behavior recurs, maintainers may remove content, warn a participant, or restrict their participation temporarily or permanently.

This code draws on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and the [RocksDB Code of Conduct](https://github.com/facebook/rocksdb/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
